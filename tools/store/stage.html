What makes an extension feel invisible

By The Editors · March 4, 2024 · 6 min read

The best extensions are resilient. They survive redesigns, they survive slow networks, and they survive the day a service quietly changes its response format — all without ever asking you to think about them.

A good extension does one thing, does it quickly, and leaves the page exactly as it found it.

A browser extension is a small software module that customizes the browser. Extensions are built with the same web technologies as ordinary pages, but they run with privileges that a page never gets, such as reading data from another origin.

How the pieces fit together

  • The content script runs inside the page and can read or change the DOM.
  • The service worker runs in the background and makes the network requests.
  • The popup and the options page are ordinary pages shipped inside the extension.

Everything else is restraint. Ask for the narrowest permissions that still do the job, keep the interface out of the way until it is wanted, and put the page back the way you found it.

让浏览器扩展「消失」的那些设计

编辑部 · 2024 年 3 月 4 日 · 6 分钟读完

做得好的扩展有一个共同点：它们足够皮实。改版扛得住，网络慢扛得住，某个服务哪天 悄悄换了返回格式也扛得住。人工智能翻译近几年把这件事的 下限抬高了不少，但把结果安静地递到眼前，仍然是设计问题而不是模型问题。

浏览器扩展是一个用来定制浏览器的小型软件模块。它用的是和普通网页一样的技术， 却跑在网页永远拿不到的权限之上。

好的扩展只做一件事，做得够快，并且把页面原样留在它来时的样子。

克制才是重点

只要能把事情办成，权限就该要得越窄越好；界面在没人叫它之前就该待着别动。真正难的 不是把功能做出来，而是决定哪些功能不做。

说到底，一个扩展值不值得留在浏览器里，取决于你有没有在不需要它的时候想起过它。 最好的答案是没有。
